# SpicyHome official building research

Observed September 7, 2026. These are building prospects, not live unit inventory. Website amenity claims do not establish parking-space or charger availability.

## Price interpretation

- Exact verified base prices within the requested $1,200–$3,000 range: AMLI 900 ($2,507), AMLI Lofts ($2,663), AMLI West Loop ($2,765).
- The Elle ($2,526) and The Grand Central ($2,548) publish total monthly starting prices. Their base amounts were not separated in the retrieved content.
- Aspire ($2,295), Coast ($2,654; 15-month lease), and 215 West ($2,683) publish monthly prices without explicit fee treatment in retrieved content. Do not relabel as base.
- Marlowe: no verified official rent obtained; availability conflicts between the floor-plan overview and A8 detail page.
- 73 East Lake: observed base price begins at $3,104, above the ceiling; keep in an optional watchlist rather than exact matches.

## Parking and EV limitations

- EV stations are advertised by AMLI Lofts, AMLI 900, AMLI West Loop, Aspire, The Elle, Grand Central, Coast and Marlowe.
- 215 West and 73 East Lake: EV remains unknown. The Elle parking terms remain unknown despite its EV amenity claim.
- 215 West advertises unreserved heated garage spaces for $300/month. Other parking/EV prices are unknown.
- Coast advertises SWTCH Level 2 stations; the garage operator states EV charging is for residents only.
- All actual space/charger availability, station condition, connector types, reservation policies and waitlists remain unverified.
- Coordinates have been left null. Use address-based map search links instead of invented pins.
